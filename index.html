Who We Are

Building digital power across Africa.

Tukonnect Digital Hub is a Kenya-based organisation working at the intersection of technology, inclusion, and community. We launched in 2025 out of a simple observation: digital tools, AI, and the conversations shaping them are largely happening without the people who need them most.

We exist to change that. We support LGBTQI+ communities, women in tech, and young emerging leaders across Kenya and East Africa to build digital skills, understand their rights in digital spaces, shape AI policy conversations, and step into their power as the next generation of thought leaders.

We started in Kisumu. We are growing across the region. The work we do here matters everywhere.

Vision
🔭

Where we're going

East Africa where communities most often left out — LGBTQI+ people, women, and young emerging leaders — are not just users of technology, but informed, protected, and powerful participants in shaping it. Where AI and digital systems reflect the dignity of every person, and where no one loses their rights, safety, or voice in a digital world.

Mission
🎯

What we're here to do

Tukonnect Digital Hub builds the digital knowledge, safety, and advocacy power of marginalised communities across Kenya and East Africa. We grow AI literacy that goes beyond tools to help people understand how these systems can protect or endanger them. We build data skills that help communities tell their own stories and hold power to account. And we bring community voices into the national and regional policy conversations shaping Africa's digital future, starting with AI governance.

Focus Areas

Four areas. One community.

Our programmes are designed with and for the communities we serve. Building digital knowledge, safety, and advocacy power across Kenya and East Africa.

01
💻
AI Literacy & Digital Skills

We run accessible, community-centred training on digital tools and artificial intelligence. We go beyond how to use AI to help people understand how these systems can protect or endanger them. Our sessions are designed for those who have been told tech is not for them: women, LGBTQI+ individuals, and young people across Kenya and East Africa.

02
📊
Data Literacy & Community Storytelling

Communities across Kenya are increasingly subjects of data collection, yet rarely understand how their data is used or how to use data themselves. We build the skills for communities to collect, read, and use data for their own advocacy, storytelling, and accountability work.

03
🏛️
AI Policy & Digital Rights Advocacy

Kenya and East Africa's AI governance landscape is being shaped right now, and community voices are largely absent from those conversations. We engage with AI policy, platform accountability, and digital rights from a human rights perspective, and we help our communities participate meaningfully in the national and regional processes that will affect their lives.

04
🌱
Fellowships & Leadership Development

Through YELP (the Young and Emerging Leaders Project) we invest in the next generation of informed, values-driven leaders across East Africa. Fellows receive skills, mentorship, and a platform to drive change in their own communities over a six-month programme.
