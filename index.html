About Us

We exist because the gap is real.

Tukonnect Digital Hub was born out of a simple but urgent observation: digital tools, AI systems, and the conversations shaping them are largely happening without the people who need them most. LGBTQI+ and gender-diverse communities across Kenya are not missing from these spaces by accident. They have been left out by design.

We exist to change that. We are a Kenya-based organisation working at the intersection of AI, digital rights, and community, building the skills, safety, and advocacy power of gender-diverse people so they can participate in, shape, and hold accountable the systems that affect their lives.

We started in Kisumu. We are growing across Kenya. The name Tukonnect comes from everyday Swahili-English slang for "let's connect." It is the kind of language people actually use, casual and warm, the way a friend reaches across to pull you into something. That informality is deliberate. This is not a gatekept institution. It is an open hand.

Programme Pillars

Four pillars.
One community.

Our programmes are designed with and for gender-diverse communities across Kenya, building AI knowledge, digital safety, advocacy power, and a peer network that lasts.

01
💻
AI Literacy & Economic Power

Hands-on, beginner-friendly training in AI and digital tools, designed for people with no prior tech background. Participants learn to use, question, and build with AI in ways relevant to their lives and livelihoods, with pathways into tech careers and freelancing. We go beyond how to use AI to help people understand how these systems can protect or endanger them.

AI/ML Basics Kaggle Zindi Project-Based
02
🛡️
Digital Safety & Protection

A core curriculum on how AI-enabled surveillance, data harvesting, and algorithmic bias disproportionately harm LGBTQI+ and gender-diverse communities in Kenya. Participants leave with practical skills including secure communications, data privacy, and threat modelling, along with a critical understanding of the technology being used against them.

Surveillance Data Privacy Threat Modelling Digital Rights
03
🏛️
Advocacy, Governance & Policy

Training community members to engage with AI policy processes, document rights violations using digital tools, and participate in governance spaces, from national consultations to regional forums. Kenya's AI regulatory landscape is being shaped right now, and community voices must be in those rooms. We help put them there.

AI Policy SOGIESC Rights Documentation Governance
04
🌱
Mentorship & Community of Practice

A structured mentorship programme pairing participants with mentors from tech, civil society, and governance. Cohort-based learning creates peer accountability, and alumni become mentors in subsequent cycles, building a self-sustaining network over time. The community does not end when the programme does.

Mentorship Cohorts Alumni Network Peer Learning

What Makes This Different

Not a general programme.
A specific one.

  • → Most AI inclusion programmes focus on gender broadly. Tukonnect centres the specific, compounded barriers that gender-diverse people face, in accessing technology, in being safe online, and in being seen in policy spaces.
  • → Digital safety and AI surveillance are core curriculum, not an add-on. Understanding how technology can be weaponised against you is inseparable from learning how to use it.
  • → The integration of SOGIESC rights with digital rights is not supplementary. It is the foundation everything else is built on.
  • → Rooted in Kisumu, operating Kenya-wide. We are not importing a model from elsewhere. We are building one that knows this ground.
