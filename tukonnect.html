{/* About */}

Tukonnect is a blend of Swahili and English. In Swahili, "Tuko"{" "} means "we are." Combined with the English word "Connect," the name means{" "} "Let's Connect" — a call to LGBTQ folks to come together, find each other, and build something in digital spaces that was not built for them.

Tukonnect Digital Hub is a Kenya-based organization working at the intersection of digital inclusion, safety, and rights-based advocacy. Our work is built around LGBTQ folks — the communities most targeted by digital harm, most excluded from digital opportunity, and most absent from the spaces where digital policy is shaped.

We are based in Kisumu, rooted in East Africa, and oriented toward a digital future that is safe and equitable for everyone, regardless of gender identity, sexual orientation, or background.

Our team brings together years of civil society practice, programme management, community organizing, and digital rights expertise.

{/* Mission / Vision */}
Mission

To advance the digital inclusion, safety, and power of LGBTQ people and women in East Africa, so they can fully participate in, shape, and benefit from the digital and AI-driven world.

Vision

A digital East Africa where LGBTQ people and women are safe online, literate in emerging technologies, represented in governance spaces, and equipped to drive the digital future on their own terms.

{/* Problem */}

LGBTQ folks face compounding barriers in digital spaces. They are disproportionately targeted by online harassment, surveillance, doxxing, and hate. They are underrepresented in AI development, digital policy, and technology leadership — and systematically excluded from the skills programmes that would allow them to protect themselves and participate meaningfully.

Why It Matters

LGBTQ people and women are not on the margins of the digital revolution. They are at the center of it.

Kenya's AI Bill, data protection frameworks, and platform policies are being finalized now. These decisions will shape digital life for decades. Tukonnect exists to make sure LGBTQ people and women have a voice in those decisions — and the tools to protect themselves in the meantime.
